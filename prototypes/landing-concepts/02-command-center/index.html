Safety is not a footnote

Confidence needs an explicit boundary.

Authorized scope only. BuildProof never implies unrestricted testing or hidden-system access.

Coverage is visible. A repository or browser target cannot prove every private runtime condition.

Evidence over assertion. AI interpretation points back to direct evidence or states what is unknown.

Fixes stay reviewable. A future auto-fix can propose a change; it cannot silently alter production.
