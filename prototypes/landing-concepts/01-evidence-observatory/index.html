Trust is part of the product

Useful findings stay within a clear boundary.

  • Only authorized repositories, environments, and test targets are assessed.
  • Coverage shows what was observed — and what remains unknown.
  • High-risk security work defaults to safe, non-destructive techniques.
  • Generated interpretation links to evidence; auto-fix means a reviewable proposal, never a silent production change.
