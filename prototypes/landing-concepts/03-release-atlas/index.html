THE BOUNDARY IS VISIBLE

Responsible assurance is explicit about its limits.

01

Authorized targets

BuildProof assesses only the repositories, environments, and accounts the customer is entitled to test.

02

Observed, not imagined

A score includes coverage and uncertainty; it cannot prove the absence of every defect or vulnerability.

03

Safe by default

High-risk security work is bounded and non-destructive unless a customer explicitly authorizes a controlled escalation.

04

Reviewable change

A future fix agent can draft a patch or review request, never make an unseen production change.
